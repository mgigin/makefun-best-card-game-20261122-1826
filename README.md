An original one-on-one card battler in the browser: you and an AI duelist each start at
30 health with a 24-card deck. Energy grows by one each turn — play creatures and spells,
send your creatures at the foe's board or hero, and bring the foe hero to zero before
yours falls. Fifteen original cards (Cinder Sprites, Thorn Golems, a Starfall Dragon,
Spark Bolts, Hearth Mends…), a full table with both hands and boards, readable costs
and combat values, synthesized sound effects, and win/loss screens. Plays offline from
static files; no build step, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Click a card in your hand to play it (or press 1–8). Click one of your ready creatures,
then a foe creature or the foe hero, to attack. Press E or the END TURN button when done.
Creatures can attack from the turn after they are played.
